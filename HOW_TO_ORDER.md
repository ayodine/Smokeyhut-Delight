# How to Order from Smokeyhut Delight

**Website:** [www.smokeyhutdelight.com](https://www.smokeyhutdelight.com)

---

## Step 1 — Visit the Homepage

When you land on the homepage you'll see:

- A **countdown timer** showing the current batch window. Orders are dispatched in four daily batches:
  - Batch 1: 10:30am – 12:30pm
  - Batch 2: 12:30pm – 2:30pm
  - Batch 3: 2:30pm – 4:30pm
  - Batch 4: 4:30pm – 6:30pm
- An **Ordering Guide popup** — read it carefully, then click **"I Understand — Let's Order!"** to dismiss it.
- A **Best Sellers** section with popular items ready to add to cart.

Click **"Order Now"** or **"View Full Menu →"** to go to the shop.

---

## Step 2 — Browse the Menu

On the **Shop** page:

1. Browse all available products — guineafowl, combos, drinks, and more.
2. Click the **+ Add to Cart** button on any product card.
3. Adjust quantity directly on the card using the **−** and **+** buttons.
4. A cart icon in the header shows how many items you've added.

---

## Step 3 — Review Your Cart

Click the **cart icon** in the navigation bar or go to `/cart`.

On the **Cart** page:
- Review each item, its quantity, and price.
- Use the **−** / **+** buttons to change quantities.
- Click the **trash icon** to remove an item.
- See your **subtotal** (delivery fee is added at checkout).
- Scroll down for **"You May Also Like"** suggestions.

When ready, click **"Proceed to Checkout →"**.

---

## Step 4 — Checkout

### 4a. Choose Delivery or Pickup

At the top of the checkout page, select one:

| Option | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Home Delivery** | We deliver to your address. Delivery fee is calculated by zone. |
| **Store Pickup** | Pick up from one of our Lagos stores — no delivery fee. |

If you choose **Store Pickup**, select your preferred store from the dropdown.

---

### 4b. Enter Your Details

Fill in the following fields:

| Field | Required |
|-------|----------|
| First Name | Yes |
| Last Name | Optional |
| Phone Number | Yes |
| Email Address | Optional (for order confirmation) |
| Delivery Address | Yes (delivery only) |
| Delivery Area | Yes (delivery only — type and select from suggestions) |
| Order Notes | Optional (e.g. extra spice, gate instructions) |

> **Tip:** For the **Delivery Area** field, start typing your area (e.g. "Lekki", "Ikeja", "Yaba") and select from the dropdown suggestions. This automatically assigns your delivery zone and fee.

---

### 4c. Apply a Coupon (Optional)

If you have a coupon code:

1. Click **"Have a coupon?"** to expand the coupon field.
2. Type your code and click **Apply**.
3. A green badge will appear with the discount amount deducted from your total.
4. To remove it, click the **✕** on the badge.

---

### 4d. Review the Order Summary

Before paying, confirm:

- **Items** and their quantities
- **Subtotal**
- **Delivery Fee** (₦0 for pickup or free-shipping items)
- **Coupon Discount** (if applied)
- **Grand Total**

---

## Step 5 — Pay

Choose your payment method:

### Option A — Card / Bank / USSD (Paystack)
1. Click **"Pay with Paystack"**.
2. A secure Paystack popup opens.
3. Pay using your debit card, bank transfer, or USSD code.
4. On success, you're taken to the **Order Confirmation** page with your order reference.

### Option B — Bank Transfer (Manual)
1. Click **"Pay via Bank Transfer"**.
2. Transfer the exact grand total to the provided bank account.
3. Your order is placed immediately as **pending** — confirmation follows once payment is verified.

---

## Step 6 — Order Confirmation

After payment you'll see a **"Order Placed!"** confirmation screen showing:

- Your order reference number (e.g. `SHD-1A2B3C`)
- Payment method used
- A summary of what you ordered

Keep this reference for tracking or any enquiries.

---

## Delivery Time

- Orders within active batch windows dispatch same day.
- Delivery takes **3–4 hours** depending on your location.
- Extended areas (Ikorodu, Lasu, Alagbado, Ojo, Ibeju-Lekki, etc.) may take up to 24 hours.
- Orders placed after the last batch (6:30pm) dispatch the **next day**.

---

## Tips for a Smooth Order

- **Be reachable** — our rider waits a maximum of 10 minutes at your location.
- **Check your package immediately** on delivery. Complaints made after 4 hours cannot be processed.
- **Double-check your phone number and address** before paying.
- **No order customisation** is available — products are prepared as listed.
- Extra packaging costs **₦1,000 per pack**.
